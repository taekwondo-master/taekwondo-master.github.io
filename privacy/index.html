TAEKWONDO MASTER Privacy Policy

Effective: app launch date | Last updated: October 31, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

TAEKWONDO MASTER (store name "Taekwondo Master: Kick & Break", the "App") respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy applies to the App on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS).

In short: There is no sign-up or login. Your training record (belts, stars, training days, dan, chosen master and any name you enter) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. When ads are shown, ad-related information (such as your advertising ID) is sent directly from your device to our ad provider, Google (Section 4).

1. Information we process

1.1 Basic use — nothing is sent to our servers

The name need not be your real name and never leaves your device unless you share a card yourself through an app you choose (messenger, social media, etc.), whose own policies then apply.

1.2 Contact

"Contact Us" in Settings opens your email app. We use your email address and message only to reply.

2. Purposes

  • Running the game: your training record is used only on your device.
  • Ads: ad-related information is used to show and measure rest-time ads and rewarded ads (heart refill, double points, retries, extra sets, unlocking masters and similar rewards), and is processed by the ad provider (Section 4).
  • Support: emails are used only to answer and to check bugs.

3. Retention

  • Training record and name: kept on your device until you delete the App or tap "Reset Progress" in Settings. We do not hold a copy.
  • Support emails: kept up to 1 year after we reply, then deleted.
  • Information required to be kept by law is kept for the required period.

4. Third parties and international transfer

We do not receive your personal information or share it with third parties. The information below goes directly from your device to the ad provider without passing through our servers.

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form on first launch and can change their choice anytime in Settings → "Ad Privacy Choices".
  • On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the game works the same either way (non-personalized ads are shown).
  • We do not provide information to third parties except as required by law or valid legal requests.

5. Processors

We do not use processors for personal information. Ads are requested directly from your device as in Section 4, and Google processes the data under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Device backup

If Android Auto Backup or iCloud Backup is on, your operating system may back up the App's training record to your Google or Apple account under their policies. We cannot access these backups.

7. Deletion

Information is deleted without delay when its retention period ends or its purpose is fulfilled; electronic files are deleted irrecoverably.

8. Your rights

  • Delete your record: use "Reset Progress" in Settings or delete the App. We have no copy, so deleted records cannot be restored.
  • Ad choices: "Ad Privacy Choices" (EEA/UK/Switzerland), resetting or opting out of your advertising ID in device settings, or turning off tracking on iOS.
  • Access/correction/deletion requests: since we hold no records, you can view and delete them on your device. To delete a support email, contact us below.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

10. Security

Your training record stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. The App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
