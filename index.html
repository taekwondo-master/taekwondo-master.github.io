Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There is no account or login. If you delete the App, your progress is deleted too.

How do I reset my progress or change my name?

Open Settings in the App and use "Reset Progress" or "My Name".

Why doesn't my belt test unlock?

Each belt needs enough stars and enough training days. A training day counts when you finish all 3 stages of any set on that day. The training map shows your training days.

Ads and tracking

The App shows ads. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the game works the same either way. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Who can play?

The App is for ages 14 and up.
